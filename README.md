# 📚QuickPrep

**QuickPrep** is an AI-powered MCQ Generator that helps you test your understanding of any study material within seconds. Simply upload your notes or paste your text, and QuickPrep will generate a personalized quiz using Google's Gemini AI.

Perfect for **last-minute revision**, **self-assessment**, and **active learning**.

---

## ✨ Features

* 📄 Upload study material as a **PDF**
* 📝 Paste any passage or notes directly into the application
* 🤖 AI-generated multiple-choice questions powered by **Google Gemini**
* ✅ Automatically generates **10 MCQs** based on the provided content
* 📊 Instant assessment with score
* ✔️ Review correct and incorrect answers after submission
* 🎯 Identify weak areas and improve your understanding quickly

---

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. Upload a PDF or paste your study material.
2. Click **Generate Quiz**.
3. You will be redirected to the assessment page.
4. Answer the 10 multiple-choice questions.
5. Click **Submit** to view:

    * Your final score
    * Correct answers
    * Incorrect answers

---

## 💡 Why QuickPrep?

Traditional revision often involves rereading notes, which can be passive and ineffective. QuickPrep transforms your study material into an interactive quiz, allowing you to actively test your knowledge and identify concepts that need more attention.

Whether you're preparing for:

* 📚 College exams
* 💼 Placement tests
* 📝 Competitive exams
* ⏰ Last-minute revision

QuickPrep helps you learn faster and retain more.

---

## 🧠 AI Powered

QuickPrep uses **Google Gemini** to generate meaningful, context-aware multiple-choice questions from your study material, making every quiz relevant to the content you provide.
